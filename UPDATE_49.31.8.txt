PROJECT ZERO V49.31.8

- Commission enemy totals are reduced to 45-50 per stage, except formal 1-1,
  which retains its original 100-enemy high-pressure design.
- Main-story Hard Mode enemies gain 5-10 additional levels based on stage,
  plus stronger HP, Shield, damage and special-resistance pressure.
- Executor archives now include identity, affiliation, profession, assignment
  status and complete combat-role information.
